Hemophage Warcasket

A new spacer-tier warcasket for [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2723801948]Vanilla Factions Expanded - Pirates[/url], made for sanguophages.

[h1]About[/h1]

The Hemophage is a 10th-generation warcasket that runs on the hemogen in its pilot's blood. It appears in no government record, and some believe sanguophages had it built in secret for themselves.

It works for any pilot with the hemogenic gene. A pilot without it gets an ordinary spacer warcasket.

[hr][/hr]
[h2]The Hemophage[/h2]
Armor, shoulder pads and helmet, welded at the warcasket foundry and unlocked by spacer warcaskets research. Mix and match the pieces with VFE Pirates' own sets as you please.
[list]
[*][b]Armor:[/b] Spends the pilot's hemogen quickly to make them much faster and stronger for a short time
[*][b]Shoulders:[/b] Tears a limb off an enemy, then drains hemogen from the wound. Holds two strikes and reloads with components
[*][b]Helmet:[/b] Shields a pilot who is sensitive to sunlight and steadies one who is afraid of fire, with the heaviest plating of the set
[/list]
Mod settings allow tuning both abilities.

[h2]Enabled work types[/h2]
VFE Pirates' warcaskets block all skilled and intellectual labor. The Hemophage's pilot keeps intellectual work, so they can still research and hack. The mod settings can block it again, or enable any of the other work a warcasket blocks.

[h1]FAQ[/h1]

[b]Q: How do I get it?[/b]
A: Research spacer warcaskets, then weld it on at the warcasket foundry like any other set.

[b]Q: Does the pilot have to be a sanguophage?[/b]
A: No. Any pilot can wear it, and any pilot with the hemogenic gene can use everything it does.
