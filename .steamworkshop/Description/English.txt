Hemophage Warcasket

A new spacer-tier warcasket for [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2723801948]Vanilla Factions Expanded - Pirates[/url], made for sanguophages.

[h1]About[/h1]

The Hemophage is an off-record 10th-generation warcasket that enhances hemogenic pilots. Rumor blames sanguophages either way: some say they stole and refitted the designs, others that it was an inside job all the way to the top.

[hr][/hr]
[h2]The Set[/h2]
Armor, shoulder pads and helmet, each with extra protection against heat damage beyond the spacer-tier baseline. Mix and match with other warcaskets' pieces as you please.
[list]
[*][b]Armor:[/b] Grants hemogen rush, allowing a pilot to accelerate their hemogen metabolism temporarily, making them much faster and stronger.
[*][b]Shoulders:[/b] Grants dismember, engaging heavy burnout servos to tear a limb clean off most enemies. A hemogenic pilot can drain hemogen even from unwilling targets this way.
[*][b]Helmet:[/b] Shields against sunlight sensitivity, and contains filters that steady against pyrophobia. Heavy plating covers a sanguophage's biggest weakness: getting their brains blown out.
[/list]

[h2]Sanguophages from quests[/h2]
Biotech's sanguophage quests can now scale to include warcaskets: both the hemophage itself, as well as lesser marine/warcasket sets on accompanying thralls can show up. Tunable in mod settings.

[h2]Enabled work types[/h2]
The pilot can still research and hack, which VFE Pirates' warcaskets otherwise block. This can be widened or narrowed in mod settings too.

[h1]FAQ[/h1]

[b]Q: How do I get it?[/b]
A: Research spacer warcaskets, then weld it on at the warcasket foundry like any other set.

[b]Q: Does the pilot have to be a sanguophage?[/b]
A: No. Any pilot with the hemogenic gene can use everything it does. Without it the pilot cannot use hemogen rush or drain hemogen when dismembering.
