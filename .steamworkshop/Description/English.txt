Hemophage Warcasket

A new spacer-tier warcasket for [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2723801948]Vanilla Factions Expanded - Pirates[/url], made for sanguophages.

[h1]About[/h1]

The Hemophage is a 10th-generation warcasket that runs on its pilot's hemogen. It appears in no government record. Some think its designs were stolen, and some believe sanguophages sanctioned it in secret from the top.

[hr][/hr]
[h2]The Set[/h2]
Armor, shoulder pads and helmet, each with extra plating against heat, welded at the warcasket foundry and unlocked by spacer warcaskets research. Mix and match the pieces with VFE Pirates' own sets as you please.
[list]
[*][b]Armor:[/b] Grants hemogen rush, which spends the pilot's hemogen quickly to make them much faster and stronger, and keeps their blood pumping even without a heart
[*][b]Shoulders:[/b] Grants dismember, which tears a limb off an enemy so a hemogenic pilot can drain hemogen from the wound. Holds a few strikes and reloads with components
[*][b]Helmet:[/b] Shields a pilot who is sensitive to sunlight and steadies one who is afraid of fire, with the heaviest plating of the set
[/list]
Mod settings allow tuning both abilities.

[h2]Enabled work types[/h2]
VFE Pirates' warcaskets block all skilled and intellectual labor. The Hemophage's pilot keeps intellectual work, so they can still research and hack. The mod settings can block it again, or enable any of the other work a warcasket blocks.

[h1]FAQ[/h1]

[b]Q: How do I get it?[/b]
A: Research spacer warcaskets, then weld it on at the warcasket foundry like any other set.

[b]Q: Does the pilot have to be a sanguophage?[/b]
A: No. Any pilot with the hemogenic gene can use everything it does. Without it the pilot cannot use the hemogen rush or drain hemogen.
